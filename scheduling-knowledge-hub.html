2The Rotation Engine — How Shifts Actually Rotate

Every team-based clinic runs on one of two fixed master templates found in the sheet.

Shift blocks

  • AM ≈ 07:30–15:30 (vets) / 07:00–16:30 (nurses)
  • MID ≈ 10:00–18:00 (vets) / 09:00–18:30 (nurses)
  • PM ≈ 14:00–22:00 (vets) / 13:00–22:30 or 00:30 (nurses; later at JVC/Palm)

4-team / 4-week master pattern (Al Wasl, Palm, JVC, BOVC)

TeamMonTueWedThuFriSatSun
Team A (Wk1)offAMAMAMAMoffoff
Team B (Wk1)PMPMPMoffMIDAMAM
Team C (Wk1)MIDMIDMIDMIDoffoffMID
Team D (Wk1)AMoffoffPMPMPMPM

Each team keeps the same days on/off — only the shift type rotates AM→MID→PM→(repeat) as the week number advances. Full cycle = 4 weeks. Net result: every team works 4 consecutive days on, then 3 off, every week.

3-team / 3-week pattern (JLT, DT, Vienna)

Identical logic, 3 teams instead of 4, cycle completes in 3 weeks. Same 4-on/3-off rule per person — but with less overlap headroom, which is exactly why these clinics sit in the "needs backfill" tier: losing one team here drops coverage much further than losing one team at a 4-team clinic.

⚠️ Saturday is structurally the thinnest day, everywhere

Confirmed independently by the rotation pattern and a prior ezyVet demand analysis (2026-08-18) — Saturday has the lowest vet headcount of the week at every multi-team clinic. Any coverage-gap tool should treat Saturday as the highest-scrutiny day by default.

2-team, 7-day clinics (Azizi, Khawaneej) — different logic

Too small for AM/MID/PM rotation — single MID shift, ~7 days/week with 1-2 fixed closed days, one vet + one nurse. Any absence = an uncovered day unless externally backfilled.

4Cover / Substitute Rules

Existing documented ruleset from prior work with ops (Mansoor), 2026-06-19/22 — vet-focused, strongest starting point available. To be validated against tomorrow's scheduling-team input.

🏆 The golden rule

Never pull a vet from a source if it drops that source below its own floor. Movement costs revenue — always exhaust the cheapest option first.

Clinic tiers

Protected

Al Wasl (MB) — never a source, never pulled from

Self-covering

JVC, Palm, BOVC — 4-team, can lend a spare 3rd

Needs-backfill

JLT, Downtown, Vienna — 3-team, zero slack

Fragile

Azizi, Khawaneej — 2-team 7-day, 1 vet/day

The day-cover ladder

  1. Internal slack (no movement): Vienna→Michaela/Flavia, BOVC→Nour
  2. Rahaf (Mobile) — lowest opportunity cost
  3. Dedicated flex cover: Ehab, then Kurt (summer: Dusko & Clara jump to top — Palm frees them)
  4. Spare 3rd from a 4-team clinic (JVC/Palm/BOVC), never below their own floor
  5. Peer day vet from another clinic, only if source stays ≥ its own floor

Never a source, ever: MB/Al Wasl vets NEVER · Irina (ultrasonographer) NEVER · Dieter (CT/MRI reserve) NEVER · any hospital/junior vet NEVER
