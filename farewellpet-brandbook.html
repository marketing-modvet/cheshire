🕊️ Brand Story

FarewellPet exists for the moment a family says goodbye. We are not a disposal service — we are the dignified, unhurried hand that carries a pet's memory forward, for the family who loved them and for the veterinary clinic who cared for them.

Positioning

A standalone, independently-branded pet funeral & cremation service, operating under Vet Alliance but built to serve every veterinary clinic in Dubai — including Vet Alliance's own competitors — with equal care and confidentiality.

Personality

Warm. Calm. Unhurried. Present. Never clinical, never salesy, never performative in its sympathy. Think of the presence of a trusted family doctor at a difficult moment — competent enough to be invisible, present enough to be felt.

🎨 Logo

Primary lock-up: circular emblem (dog + cat beneath a weeping willow branch) paired with the "Farewell" wordmark and "PET FUNERAL SERVICES" descriptor.

Clear space & minimum size

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the emblem circle on all sides — no text, edges, or other graphics inside that zone. Minimum digital width: 120px. Minimum print width: 25mm. Below this, use the emblem alone (see Logo Variants).

Logo variants needed

  • Primary (full lock-up) — website header, print collateral, signage — only source file we have today
  • Emblem only — app icons, social profile photo, favicon, small-space applications — needs to be extracted/redrawn as a clean vector, ideally in-house or with the same designer who built the original
  • White/reversed version — for use on the coral background or dark photos
  • Horizontal vs. stacked lock-up — current file is horizontal; a stacked version (emblem above wordmark) is worth having for square social formats
⚠️ We currently only have the flattened PDF/PNG of the full lock-up. Before large-format print (signage, vehicle branding) or the app-icon/social-avatar crop, get the original vector source (AI/EPS/SVG) from whoever designed it, or have it redrawn — flattened raster art will look soft at large sizes and can't be recoloured cleanly for the white/reversed variant.

🎨 Colour System

Only one official colour existed before this brand book — the coral from the logo. Everything else below is proposed to give the brand a workable system across web, print, and social while keeping coral as the unmistakable signature colour.

Neutrals & grounding tone

Ink#2B2422Primary text colour — warm black, not pure #000
Sage Tint#DDE6E1Secondary backgrounds, quiet contrast to coral tint

Usage ratio (guideline)

Roughly 10% coral, 60% warm neutral/cream, 20% ink/sage text, 10% accents. Coral is a signature, not a wash — it should mark key moments (logo, CTAs, section dividers) rather than fill entire screens, which would tip a grief-care brand into feeling alarming rather than warm.

🗣️ Voice & Tone

One rule above all: this brand speaks like a caring person, not a service provider. Every word choice either respects that or undermines it.

Instead of…Say…
Disposal / processingCare / farewell / aftercare
The body / the remainsYour pet / [pet's name] whenever known
Cremation unit / batchIndividual / private cremation
Client / customerFamily, or the pet owner's name
Order / transaction / invoice-first languageArrangement, request, "we're here to help"
Service feeCare package / arrangement cost (only when pricing must be shown)
"Thank you for your business""We're honoured you trusted us with [pet's name]"

Golden rules

  • Never rush the reader. Short, calm sentences beat long persuasive ones.
  • No graphic detail, ever — describe process in terms of care and outcome, not mechanics.
  • B2B copy (to clinics) can be more operational/direct — turnaround times, logistics, reliability — but should still open and close warmly, never purely transactional.
  • Humour has no place here. Warmth does not require levity.

📣 Content Pillars (social & website)

1. Reassurance & Education

What actually happens, is burial legal in the UAE, how to choose between service tiers, what to expect timeline-wise. Reduces anxiety at the exact moment someone is searching in distress.

2. Memorial & Remembrance (with permission)

Paw-print keepsakes, urn options, memorial certificates — always with explicit owner consent before anything is shared publicly.

3. Behind-the-Scenes Dignity

The care taken, the facility, the people (once hired) — never the equipment mid-operation, never remains. Builds trust through transparency about process, not mechanics.

4. Clinic Partner Trust (B2B-facing)

Spotlighting the partnership model, logistics reliability, and testimonials from partner clinics once they exist — this is the pillar that does the heaviest commercial lifting.
