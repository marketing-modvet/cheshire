How-To Guide

Lab Sample Transfer

Sending a sample to TVRC, Al Wasl Hospital, or Blue Oasis lab? This app tracks every sample from the moment it's taken to the moment results are in — no more "did the sample arrive?" messages.

1 · Submitting a Sample (in Slack)

1

Type the slash command

In any Slack channel or DM, type /labsample and press Enter.

2

Fill in sample details

Pick From/To clinic, patient name & ID, sample type(s), and container type(s). Use the searchable Lab Test(s) Requested field to find the exact test from our full catalog of 550+ tests — just start typing.

3

Set Collect By time (optional)

If the sample needs a driver to physically move it, fill in Collect By — Date/Time. This automatically books a driver for you — no separate dispatch request needed.

Tip: Leave it blank if the sample is already at the lab, or someone's hand-carrying it themselves.

3 · Accept / Reject / Reassign

A

Accept

The receiving lab confirms it's expecting the sample.

B

Reject

Asks for a short reason — the sender is notified immediately.

C

Reassign

Sends the sample to a different lab instead. This creates a brand new ticket at the new destination, so it goes through its own fresh Accept step.

FAQ

Why can't I find my test in a dropdown list?
The Lab Test(s) field is a search box, not a dropdown — there are over 550 tests, too many to scroll through. Just start typing the test name.
What happens if I reassign a ticket?
The old ticket is closed and a brand new one is opened at the new destination lab — it needs to be accepted fresh, same as any new ticket.
Do I need to request a driver separately for the sample?
No — fill in "Collect By" in the form and a driver task is created and posted to #driver-dispatch automatically.
