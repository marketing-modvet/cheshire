Key Takeaways

  • Google Ads drives the highest LTV/customer (3,147 AED) of any channel, ahead of Organic/Direct (2,857) and Google Maps/GMB (2,595) — despite Maps/GMB bringing in far more volume (917 vs 112 customers).
  • Emergency Daytime Campaign is the standout Google Ads performer on both fronts: highest volume (18 customers) and one of the better LTV:CAC ratios (1.06) among campaigns with meaningful spend.
  • Search ll Mobile clinic, Search ll 24 Hrs Emergency ll Day, and Search ll Dental care show very low LTV:CAC (0.02–0.04) — high spend relative to acquired-customer revenue so far. Worth a closer look at whether these are newer campaigns still maturing, or genuinely underperforming.
  • JVC General Campaign & JLT Branch show zero matched ad spend in Google Ads for this period despite acquiring 6 customers each — likely spend logged under a different campaign name/account, or these conversions came via a different attribution path. Needs reconciliation.
  • 2 VetIQ campaigns unmatched against Google Ads data ("mobile vet-2025_S_dxb_en_daytime_5.25++_CG1", "Out of Coverage") — possibly renamed/paused campaigns or a different sub-account. Flagged for follow-up.
  • Cohort maturity effect: LTV/customer peaked at 4,198 AED for the Feb 2025 cohort (most time to spend) and trends down toward 1,857 AED for Jun 2026 — this is a timing artifact, not a quality decline.
