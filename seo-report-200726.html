Executive Summary

July: Authority Up, Rankings Cooled Off

A mixed month. Domain authority signals moved firmly in the right direction — referring domains, backlinks, organic traffic and organic keywords all grew on Ahrefs — and AI visibility kept climbing, with ChatGPT responses up to 32 (+6) and AI Overviews up to 89 (+11). But head-to-head keyword rankings softened across both the mobile and desktop trackers compared to end-June, with Top 3 and Top 10 counts down and several high-volume terms slipping. Net picture: the foundation is getting stronger even though this period's rankings dipped — worth a closer look at what changed on-page or in the SERPs.

Outlook

Trend Watch & Next Steps

Top 10 / Top 3 counts softened this period 🔴

Mobile campaign: Top 10 fell from 95 → 88, Top 3 from 39 → 26, and #1 positions from 19 → 9 vs the June 30 snapshot. Average position slipped from 12.1 to 13.3. This is the main thing to investigate — check for SERP layout changes, new competitors, or any recent on-page edits around late June/early July.

High-volume "veterinary clinic dubai" dropped out of top 10 ⚠️

Fell 12 positions to #27 (mobile) and 20 positions to #37 (desktop) — a 1,900-search-volume term worth prioritising for a content/on-page refresh.

"animal hospital" and "veterinary hospital" both slipped ⚠️

animal hospital: −15 to #24 (mobile); veterinary hospital: −14 to #23 (mobile), −13 to #15 desktop territory. Both are core commercial terms — worth a quick technical/content check on the pages ranking for these.

Domain authority is genuinely strengthening ✅

Referring domains +62, backlinks +321, organic traffic +237, organic keywords +29 — all up on Ahrefs this period, with Domain Rating holding steady at 53. This is the underlying asset that compounds over time even when short-term rank positions wobble.

AI/GEO channel is expanding ✅

ChatGPT visibility continues to grow (32 responses, +6) alongside AI Overviews (89, +11). No action needed immediately, but this channel is worth watching monthly as it's growing distinctly from classic SEO rankings.

Some standout individual gains this period ✅

"vet clinic" and "veterinary clinic" (2,900 vol each) both jumped into the top 3 (+20 and +18 positions). "the vet clinic" gained +85 positions. "cat vaccinations" gained +59. These show the content/authority work is still landing wins even in a softer overall month.
