Summary & Prognosis

July 2026 came in at 6,511,251 AED vs 6,664,998 AED in June — a decline of just 2.3% (5.5% on a daily-average basis, adjusting for July's extra day). This is confirmed actual data, replacing the earlier 6.40–6.50M projection — and it landed even milder than that projection suggested.

The seasonal July dip keeps shrinking every year — 2026 is now a confirmed actual, and it's the mildest yet:
  • 2024: −12.0% (daily −14.8%)
  • 2025: −6.0% (daily −9.1%)
  • 2026: −2.3% (daily −5.5%) — actual, confirmed
Each year the summer trough gets shallower — demand is becoming more resilient to the low season, and this year's number came in even better than the pacing suggested.

YoY growth is accelerating. June 2026 grew +20.3% vs 2025 (which itself grew +9.4% vs 2024). July 2026 came in at +25.1% YoY — confirmed, not projected — meaning our "slowest" month this summer still beat last July by a full quarter, and comfortably outpaced any single month of summer 2025.

August prognosis (only remaining forward-looking figure): August has recovered from July in both prior years — +8.3% in 2024 and +4.1% in 2025. Applying that same recovery range to the now-confirmed July actual gives August 2026 at 6.78–7.05M AED (daily average 218,700–227,500), i.e. +25.1% to +30.2% YoY. The midpoint scenario (~6.9M) would make it the strongest month of the summer and likely a new record for an August.

Bottom line: With June and July now both confirmed actuals, this summer is tracking meaningfully ahead of the earlier pacing — the seasonal softening was milder than projected, and YoY growth came in stronger than projected too. August remains the only open month, and the same recovery pattern from the past two years points to a record summer close.
