Summary & Prognosis

July 2026 came in at 6,511,251 AED vs 6,664,998 AED in June — a decline of just 2.3% (5.5% on a daily-average basis, adjusting for July's extra day). This is confirmed actual data, replacing the earlier 6.40–6.50M projection — and it landed even milder than that projection suggested.

The seasonal July dip keeps shrinking every year — 2026 is now a confirmed actual, and it's the mildest yet:
  • 2024: −12.0% (daily −14.8%)
  • 2025: −6.0% (daily −9.1%)
  • 2026: −2.3% (daily −5.5%) — actual, confirmed
Each year the summer trough gets shallower — demand is becoming more resilient to the low season, and this year's number came in even better than the pacing suggested.

YoY growth is accelerating. June 2026 grew +20.3% vs 2025 (which itself grew +9.4% vs 2024). July 2026 came in at +25.1% YoY — confirmed, not projected — meaning our "slowest" month this summer still beat last July by a full quarter, and comfortably outpaced any single month of summer 2025.

August base case (historical seasonal pattern): August has recovered from July in both prior years — +8.3% in 2024 and +4.1% in 2025. Applying that same recovery range to the now-confirmed July actual gives August 2026 at 6.78–7.05M AED (daily average 218,700–227,500), i.e. +25.1% to +30.2% YoY. The midpoint scenario (~6.9M) would make it the strongest month of the summer and likely a new record for an August — if the recovery follows the same shape as the last two years.
Pessimistic scenario — a delayed, not lost, recovery: the regional conflict this year is a genuine wildcard the historical pattern can't account for. The specific risk isn't that demand disappears — it's that the usual Jul→Aug snap-back gets pushed back roughly a month if residents/expats who would normally have already returned from summer travel delay coming back (fewer pets back in the country, fewer routine visits resuming on schedule). That would show up as August looking more like a continuation of July's flat pattern rather than a rebound.
  • Downside case: August lands at 6.20–6.45M AED (roughly flat to −5% vs July, instead of the usual +4–8% bounce) — still +14.5% to +19.0% YoY, because the YoY base is so strong, but a meaningfully softer month than base case and a clear deceleration vs July's own +25.1% YoY pace.
  • Why this magnitude, not worse: anchored to the weakest recovery we've actually seen (2025's Aug was only +4.1% over July, the softest of the two comparable years) and shaded further down — not modeled as an outright collapse, because GA4 traffic/conversion data for July 2026 shows a steady, flat week-over-week pattern with no sign yet of the kind of sharp drop-off seen in the same window in 2025 (weekly conversions fell from ~700/week to ~240/week by early August 2025, likely tied to last year's regional escalation). If this year's disruption effect is simply delayed rather than avoided, that kind of late-summer air-pocket is the realistic worst case for August rather than September.
  • What would confirm this scenario early: a visible session/conversion dip in the first 1–2 weeks of August (vs the flat July pattern), or anecdotal signals of delayed expat return (school-year start dates, flight booking patterns, clinic no-show/reschedule rates ticking up).

Bottom line: With June and July now both confirmed actuals, this summer is tracking meaningfully ahead of the earlier pacing — the seasonal softening was milder than projected, and YoY growth came in stronger than projected too. August remains the only open month. The base case (same recovery pattern as the past two years) points to a record summer close around 6.78–7.05M. The pessimistic case — a conflict-delayed recovery rather than a lost one — puts August closer to 6.20–6.45M, still comfortably up YoY but a real step down from the base case and from July's own growth rate. Worth tracking weekly rather than waiting for month-end to find out which scenario is playing out.
