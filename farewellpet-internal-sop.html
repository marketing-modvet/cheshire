How the 11 Vet Alliance clinics refer to FarewellPet — for review & refinement by the ops team

⚠️ Draft status

This is a first-pass draft built from what's already known about the process and the existing dispatch system. It needs review from whoever runs day-to-day clinic operations before it's rolled out as official policy — flagged sections below need real input.

Call flow: when a pet passes at a clinic

1

Vet/nurse informs the family of aftercare options

Explain the FarewellPet service tiers available (private/individual, communal, memorial add-ons) — using current placeholder framing until final pricing/tiers are confirmed.

"We work with FarewellPet for aftercare arrangements. They offer a few different options depending on what feels right for you and [pet's name] — would you like me to explain them, or would you prefer some time first?"
2

Family makes a decision (or reception follows up later)

No pressure to decide immediately — reception can note the case and follow up within a set window window TBC by ops if the family needs time.

3

Clinic logs the case & requests pickup

Using the existing driver-dispatch system (already proven across the 11-clinic network) — same request flow as lab samples/other inter-clinic logistics, routed to FarewellPet instead of another clinic. Integration TBC — see note below

4

FarewellPet collects, cares for, and returns (if applicable)

Ashes/keepsake return follows the family's chosen tier and timeline — clinic is notified so they can inform the family, or FarewellPet contacts the family directly TBC — who owns family communication?

5

Clinic follow-up (optional, recommended)

A short, warm check-in from the clinic days later reinforces care continuity — optional but strongly recommended as part of the client experience.

Staff talking points — what to say, what to avoid

SituationSayAvoid
Explaining options"There are a few ways we can help care for [pet's name] afterwards.""You need to decide what to do with the body."
Discussing cost"The arrangement cost depends on the option you choose — I can get you the details."Quoting exact prices before they're confirmed — defer to "I'll confirm and get back to you."
If family is distressedGive space, slow down, no rushing toward a decision.Any hint of urgency about logistics/paperwork in that moment.
If asked "is this a Vet Alliance thing?""FarewellPet is an independent aftercare service we work with — they handle it with a lot of care."Over-explaining the corporate relationship; keep it simple and warm.
