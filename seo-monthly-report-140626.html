Headline

This was a breakout month. Over the 30-day window, mobile Top 10 keywords nearly tripled — 32 → 93 (of 144 tracked), and Top 3 more than tripled — 10 → 32. Average ranked position improved 20.8 → 15.4, and the unranked bucket shrank from 19 to 5.

The single most important move: "vet near me" (vol 5,400) climbed from #24 to #2 — the most valuable organic position the tracker covers. Around it, the entire commercial head-term family consolidated onto page 1. The three mid-volume terms flagged as "watch / decliner" in the 15 May brief — pet hospital dubai, vet care, vet veterinary clinic — all recovered strongly this month.
