What we can run for you

Routine and specialised diagnostics across six core areas.

Haematology & Biochemistry

CBC & blood smear assessment, reticulocyte counts, comprehensive & extended canine/feline profiles, electrolytes and individual parameters.

Microbiology

Routine, faecal and blood cultures with antibiotic sensitivity testing, plus dermatophyte, fungal and yeast cultures.

Cytology & Microscopy

Synovial fluid & CSF analysis, hair and skin scrape examination, Gram, rapid and specialised stains.

Immunology & Parasitology

Rapid testing incl. Parvo, Distemper, Giardia, FeLV/FIV and canine 4DX; faecal flotation, Baermann testing and blood parasite detection.

Urine Diagnostics

Dipstick testing, full urinalysis, sediment examination, urine culture and UPC ratio.

Avian & Exotic Diagnostics

Dedicated falcon, parrot and reptile biochemistry profiles — a rarity in the region.

Also available: specific & endocrine assays (cPLi/fPLi, cortisol, SDMA, Total T4, CRP, phenobarbital and more) and canine/feline VacciCheck serology.
