02 · The problem

You can't advertise a hip replacement like a vaccine.

Owners don't search for the procedure

Nobody wakes up wanting a THR. They search "dog limping", "hip dysplasia Labrador", "my dog can't get up". Direct-to-owner demand for the surgery itself is tiny and expensive to buy.

Referrals are where the volume is

Most THR cases arrive because another vet says "you need a specialist." Very few clinics in the UAE can offer this. That makes referring vets our real customer.

The catch

Referring clinics are afraid of us

Modern Vet is a 7-location general-practice group. A GP vet's fear is specific and rational: "I send my client for surgery, and a month later they're getting vaccine reminders from Modern Vet."

No amount of surgical excellence fixes that. Only a credible, kept promise does.

04 · The positioning

We don't hide Modern Vet. We own it.

Dr. Moustafa is on the Modern Vet website, on LinkedIn, and every vet in Dubai knows TVRC and Modern Vet are one group. Presenting him as "pure TVRC" would be found out in one Google search — and look like we're hiding something.

So the position is simple and true: Modern Vet surgeon. Referral cases via TVRC, under TVRC's rules.

What we say to a sceptical referring vet
"Yes, he's a Modern Vet surgeon — we're not hiding that. That's exactly why we route referrals through TVRC: so there's a formal firewall, in writing, between your client and our GP clinics."

Owning the weakness turns it into the reason to trust us.

05 · The firewall

The TVRC Referral Charter — five promises we keep.

  1. The patient goes back to you.

    TVRC handles the surgery and immediate post-op only. Rechecks, suture removal, rehab, medication, everything else returns to the referring vet. We say it to the owner at discharge, out loud.

  2. You get everything, fast.

    Surgical report, discharge summary and radiographs within 24 hours. A phone call — not an email — for any complication. You should look smarter to your client because you referred.

  3. Zero marketing to your client. Ever.

    Referred owners are never added to Klaviyo/Mailchimp, never sent booking links, reminders or clinic leaflets. Built into the intake process so it can't be broken by accident.

  4. Shared credit.

    Case studies and videos say "referred by Dr. X at Y Clinic". The referring practice gets to post it too. Referring to us makes them look good.

  5. In writing, in public.

    The charter lives on the TVRC website where referring vets will see it, and is handed over on the first visit. A promise you can point to beats one you have to remember.

06 · Making it real

A promise is a poster. A process is a firewall.

One flag in ezyVet at booking — "TVRC referral" — drives every rule automatically.

Intake

Flag at booking

Referral form → coordinator books → patient tagged "TVRC referral", referring vet + clinic recorded.

Marketing

Auto-exclude

Flag suppresses Klaviyo/Mailchimp enrolment, review requests, reminders and any Modern Vet leaflet in the discharge pack.

Clinical

Report in 24h

Discharge triggers the surgical report + rads to the referring vet. Complications = a phone call from the surgeon.

Discharge

Send them home — to you

Script: "Your vet at [clinic] will take over from here." Follow-up is booked with the referrer, not with us.

Owner: a named referral coordinator (reception/CX) who lives this process. Audit it monthly — one leaked reminder email undoes a year of trust.
