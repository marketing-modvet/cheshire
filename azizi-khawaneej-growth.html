4 · Azizi Riviera plan

From launch burst to always-on engine

1
Win back the trial cohort — cheapest revenue in the group.

~250–300 Feb–May visits sit in ezyVet with contacts. Vaccine-due reminders, "we miss you" offer, wellness-plan pitch. Cost ≈ zero; these clients already chose us once (even if panic-driven — they know the door).

2
Shift hours to the evening — cost-neutral.

Same 8 hours, same one vet: ~12:30–8:30pm. We become the only clinic in the development open when residents get home (6:30–7:30pm). Data shows demand still rising at the current close.

3
Switch on always-on local search — AED 2.5–3K/mo.

3km radius: “vet near me / vet meydan / vet azizi riviera”, ad schedule matched to opening hours. Same treatment Palm and JVC already get.

4
Make the single vet the product.

“Your personal vet — the same doctor who knows your pet, every visit.” Continuity of care is the one thing rotating-team competitors structurally can't claim. Vet-fronted reels, building-community content, promenade presence.

5
Deepen partnerships beyond ELLO.

Audit what ELLO produced; replicate with groomers, pet shops, community management (building WhatsApp groups, resident events). Floof delivery cross-sell into the same towers.

6
Retention product: wellness plan / membership pilot.

Azizi is the ideal small pilot for the membership programme once pre-approved — converts trial traffic into recurring revenue and stops the churn pattern repeating.

Target: 120–180 pets/mo ≈ 60–90K AED/mo by Q1 2027 (post-summer). Well inside one vet's capacity. Judge new campaigns from September, not August — summer suppresses everything.
5 · Khawaneej plan

Arabic-first acquisition to 50–80K — while preparing the crematorium

1
Make Al Ttay a destination — free, do first.

The clinic sits in a warehouse district — so we sell the trip, not just the clinic. The striking blue Modern Vet facade becomes the visual anchor: lead every GMB photo, ad and post with it, add landmark directions from Al Khawaneej Rd, full Arabic descriptions and weekly posts. Destination businesses thrive in warehouse districts (gyms, padel courts) — clients just need total confidence they're headed to the right place.

2
Reactivate the paused local campaign + Arabic campaign — AED 2.5–3K/mo.

Radius covering Khawaneej / Al Mizhar / Al Ttay / Mirdif east; Arabic ad copy and landing page (the Arabic page got ~10 views in 90 days). Schedule matched to Sat–Wed hours; respect Friday.

3
Review velocity programme.

Every visit → rate-us ask (page already exists). Target ★4.8+, 150+ reviews in 6 months. The Moon Vet wins on social proof, not medicine.

4
Mobile clinic as villa feeder.

The mobile clinic is the group's cheapest converter (≈ AED 3.5/conversion on video campaigns). Route it through Khawaneej/Mizhar villas; every home visit introduces the local clinic for follow-ups.

5
Community embedment, Emirati-appropriate.

Partnerships with local majlis events, schools, pet shops; Arabic-speaking staff visibility; word-of-mouth referral incentive (refer-a-neighbour).

6
Crematorium: build the B2B launch in parallel — different playbook.

Customers = every clinic in Dubai (competitors become referral accounts). Anchor volume = our own 11 clinics' end-of-life cases. Own the “pet cremation Dubai” search (high intent, almost uncontested). Dignified sub-brand, memorial products, transport on our existing driver/dispatch rails. Clinic acquisition above stays lean and reversible so spend isn't stranded if the site's identity changes.

Target: 100–160 pets/mo ≈ 50–80K AED/mo for the single vet by Q1 2027 — matching the stated goal.
