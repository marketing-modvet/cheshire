Emerging Channel

AI Search Visibility (GEO)

Modern Vet is appearing in ChatGPT recommendations

As of June 26, 2026, ChatGPT is actively recommending Modern Vet Hospital as one of Dubai's top veterinary clinics when users ask for "best vet in Dubai" type queries. This is a significant milestone — Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) is rapidly becoming a real traffic and brand-awareness channel as AI assistants increasingly answer queries that would previously drive Google clicks.

Our GA4 data already shows an "AI Assistant" acquisition channel with a 69% engagement rate and meaningful conversions — confirming that AI-driven traffic converts well. Modern Vet's strong content, brand authority (DR 53), and near-me keyword dominance are the likely reasons for inclusion.

Outlook

Trend Watch & Next Steps

High-volume near-me terms are consolidating ✅

"Veterinary clinic near me" (1,900 searches) and "Pet clinic near me" (1,300 searches) have broken into the top 10. These are the most commercially valuable keyword moves of the month. Continue monitoring for further consolidation into top 3.

Share of Voice is accelerating ✅

SoV grew from ~5.5% to 15.38% across June — nearly tripling in 4 weeks. Est. Traffic Share hit 19.19%. This reflects both ranking improvements and competitor stagnation. Sustain with consistent content publishing.

High-volume generic terms need sustained push ⚠️

"Vet" (12,100 searches) is at position 34, "veterinary clinic" (6,600) is at 54. These terms are improving but require continued on-page and authority work to break into the top 10. They are long-term targets.

"The Veterinary Hospital Dubai" drop needs monitoring ⚠️

This branded competitor term dropped 47 positions to #66. This could indicate SERP reshuffling in the hospital/emergency space. Worth watching — if it recovers, consider whether our emergency landing page content needs strengthening.

AI/GEO channel is live and converting ✅

ChatGPT is now recommending Modern Vet. GA4 shows this channel has a 69% engagement rate. No action needed immediately — but ensuring structured data, E-E-A-T signals, and FAQ/review content remains fresh will help maintain and expand this visibility.

Cat-specific keywords underperforming 🔴

"Cat vet dubai" dropped 11 positions to #25. With Vienna Vet (cat-friendly, ISFM Gold) in the group, there's an opportunity to build dedicated cat-focused landing content on modernvet.com or cross-link from Vienna Vet to capture this segment.
