Photo & Video Shot List

For the FarewellPet photographer/videographer — everything below can be scheduled and shot today

⚠️ Hard rules — read first

  • Nothing graphic, ever. No deceased animals, no equipment mid-operation, no cremation chamber in use.
  • No identifiable client information in frame (no visible names/paperwork on desks unless staged/blank).
  • Tone is warm and dignified in every frame — nothing clinical, nothing that looks like a factory or an industrial process.
  • Get a signed release from any staff member who appears on camera before publishing anything.
  • If in doubt about whether a shot crosses a line, don't take it — flag it and ask first.

📸 Stills — can shoot now

The facility, exterior & entrance

The FarewellPet entrance to the Khawaneej building (separate from the clinic entrance) — establishing shot for website/social, ideally in soft morning or late-afternoon light.

Interior — reception / waiting area

Calm, warm, softly lit. If furniture/decor isn't finished yet, shoot what exists now as a placeholder and re-shoot once finished — don't wait for a perfect room.

Memorial & keepsake products

Urns, paw-print kits, memorial certificates, any keepsake items already on hand — flat-lay or styled product shots, soft natural light, coral/cream colour backdrop where possible per the Brand Book.

Detail / texture shots

Close-ups of fabric, wood grain, candlelight, a single flower — abstract, calm imagery useful as website/social backgrounds and section dividers. Doesn't need people or pets — can be shot anywhere, anytime.

Brand/logo in context

Signage, any printed material with the logo, the building entrance signage once installed.

📸 Stills — once staff are hired later

Team portraits

Warm, approachable headshots and a few candid "at work" shots (answering phone, greeting a family at reception) — never near equipment or mid-process.
