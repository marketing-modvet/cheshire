Why these two

Small, unglamorous problems that quietly cost you.

Both apps target the same failure mode: something needed a human, and nobody was nudged. Both reuse the Slack bot, tokens, and cron infrastructure already running your other automations — nothing new to install, log into, or maintain separately.

01

Inquiries go quiet

A question gets asked in a channel or a DM, and without a reminder, it's easy for it to simply scroll away unanswered.

02

Good numbers, no visibility

Doctors driving strong subscription conversions have no easy way to see their own performance without someone manually pulling and sending it.

03

Same toolkit, twice

Both run as scheduled jobs on infrastructure you already have — the Slack bot used for onboarding and dispatch, extended, not replaced.

App 01

Unanswered Inquiry Reminder

Watches for questions directed at a specific person or a channel's designated owner, and if it goes unanswered past a set window, quietly DMs a reminder to whoever's on the hook — never posted publicly, never a scold, just a nudge.

1
Inquiry detectedA channel message @-mentions someone, or lands in a channel with a configured owner.
2
Clock startsPer-channel rule applies — 24h flat for round-the-clock teams (reception, call centre), or 2h counted only within working hours elsewhere.
3
Still quiet at deadlineNo thread reply from anyone but the original poster.
4
DM reminder sentResponsible person gets a private nudge with a direct link to the message — once, not repeatedly.

Where this stands

  • Design + threshold logic locked with Andrew
  • Config format drafted (channel → owner → rule)
  • Waiting: channel list + responsible people, per channel
  • Waiting: confirm event-driven vs. polling for open @-mentions workspace-wide

App 02

Doctor Subscription Report

Every week, each doctor gets a private DM with their own subscription-conversion numbers — pulled straight from the data source Andrew connects, mapped to their Slack account automatically.

1
Data source connectsSheet, database, or API supplying subscription counts per vet, per period.
2
Weekly job runsPulls the latest closed period's numbers for every doctor on the list.
3
Matched to SlackSame email → Slack ID lookup already used for clinic onboarding.
4
Personal DM deliveredEach doctor sees only their own numbers — nothing broadcast to a channel.

Where this stands

  • Cadence agreed — weekly to start, easy to change
  • DM format drafted, ready to adjust to real fields
  • Waiting: which data source Andrew is connecting
  • Waiting: exact columns/row shape from that source
