Al Khawaneej — Analytics & Plan

Response to the Chairman's mall-advertising request · revenue reality, offline ad cost benchmarking, and the right-sized reactivation plan agreed Aug 11–12, 2026

✅ The diagnosis is right. The proposed fix is not.

The Chairman is correct that awareness is Al Khawaneej's problem, not capacity — the clinic is running at roughly 10% utilization with an almost-empty appointment book. But the requested fix (mall banners, digital screens, trolley ads across 2-3 malls, plus flyers) realistically costs AED 15,000–45,000 per month — more than the clinic's entire monthly revenue (AED 6,900–8,700). Spending more on awareness than the clinic earns, every month, cannot pay for itself on Khawaneej's own volume alone.

The honest, cheap fix was already sitting there: two Google Ads campaigns built specifically for Khawaneej have been paused this whole time, at zero cost to turn back on. Decision taken: reactivate digital + one targeted villa flyer drop for AED 4,000 total, hold mall/trolley media as a funded phase 2 once demand actually grows.

The right-sized plan — approved & in motion

  • 1
    Turn the switch that's already off. "Al Khawaneej Local Campaign" and "Arabic Campaign" on Google Ads were both paused with zero Khawaneej-specific ad spend running. Reactivated at a modest budget — directly targets people actively searching nearby, far cheaper per lead than mall media. ~AED 0 incremental to turn on
  • 2
    One targeted villa flyer drop, not a mall campaign. Villa communities immediately around Al Ttay/Khawaneej — matches the "analog audience exists" instinct behind the original ask, but scoped and priced correctly. ~AED 2,500–4,000 one-time
  • 3
    Cheap physical visibility at the clinic itself instead of a mall 10-15 minutes away — street-facing signage + stronger Google Maps/GMB destination signal. ~AED 500–2,000 one-time
  • 4
    Hold mall/trolley media as phase 2, funded only once the clinic actually grows into the Q1 2027 target (100-160 pets/mo ≈ AED 50-80K/mo) — proportionate spend once the clinic can absorb the acquisition cost. Not yet funded

Decision taken (Aug 11-12, 2026): resumed the Khawaneej campaign Aug 12 — geo-targeted social media, total budget AED 4,000 (the max affordable at this clinic's current size), target ROI 5-8x. Flyers remain in scope; real vendor quotes being gathered. Display boxes and banners explicitly ruled out of scope for this phase.

Timing caveat: August is the lowest-demand month of the year for vet visits in Dubai (summer low season) — flat or modest early results should not be read as the campaign underperforming. September is the right baseline month to judge real traction.

Methodology & sources: internal ezyVet / Google Ads / GA4 pulls (Jun–Jul 2026), Dubai outdoor/mall advertising rate guides (2026, various UAE agencies — indicative market ranges, not signed quotes), Bayut/area profiles for Mirdif/Al Khawaneej, live web search for Synergy Vet's actual presence. Offline ad rate figures are indicative, not live vendor quotes — real quotes are being gathered before any paid mall/trolley commitment is made. Internet penetration context: UAE ~99% (DataReportal, Digital 2026) — the "analog-only, unreachable through digital" audience is a defensible low-single-digit slice of the local population, which is part of why the phased digital-first plan was chosen over offline media.
