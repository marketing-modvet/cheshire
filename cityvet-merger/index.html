Merging with The City Vet Clinic:
brand architecture, marketing budget and team scenarios

What City Vet actually brings, where it sits against our own clinics, and four ways to absorb it — each costed for monthly marketing spend, one-off integration cost and headcount. Prepared by Marketing from public data, our own market database and the approved September 2026 budget — which covers the whole Vet Alliance group (Modern Vet, Blue Oasis, Vienna Vet, TVRC, Blue Sky, Floof), not Modern Vet alone. No City Vet financials were available; every assumption is listed and can be swapped for real numbers.

Executive summary

9City Vet branches: 7 Dubai + Abu Dhabi + Al Ain. Largest chain competitor in Dubai.
20Combined network after merger (our 11 units + 9). Nearly doubles the estate.
4.52★City Vet weighted Google rating, 3,331 reviews (ours: 4.74★, 8,401 reviews).
+28–56%Group marketing budget uplift across scenarios: from AED 223K to AED 285–347K/month. One brand ≈ +28%; keeping City Vet as a brand ≈ +56%.

Recommendation: Scenario 4 — "Modern Vet everywhere, one flagship repurposed"

Rebrand 8 of the 9 City Vet clinics to Modern Vet in waves (East Dubai and the two new emirates first), and treat the Al Wasl flagship — 800 m from Modern Vet Hospital — as a repurposing decision (Vienna Vet #2, a Modern Vet wellness/grooming/exotics hub, or consolidation into the Hospital) rather than a second GP clinic under a third brand. Run "City Vet — now part of Vet Alliance" endorsement branding as the bridge: 6 months in Dubai, 12 months in Abu Dhabi and Al Ain where City Vet has eight years of awareness and Modern Vet has none.

  • Why not keep City Vet as a brand (Scenario 2): it's the cheapest one-off (~AED 145K) but the most expensive forever — a permanent third GP brand costs ~AED 124K/month more (vs ~65K for one brand): a second website to rank, separate ad accounts, its own production and collateral, an operations specialist and ~25% raises for the specialists who would run two brands. It splits our paid search against ourselves in 4 neighbourhoods, and its digital estate (DR 20, 229 organic visits/mo, duplicate socials, dead shop) is weaker than ours in every measurable way. We'd be paying to maintain the weaker asset.
  • Why not the pure hybrid (Scenario 3, keep City Vet where we overlap): it costs almost the same per month as keeping the whole brand (+AED 121K) and adds a rebrand one-off on top — two brands competing 0.8–2.6 km apart on Al Wasl, in JVT and in Meydan, with two websites, two ad accounts and two social stacks kept alive to protect three clinics.
  • Why Scenario 4 over the straight "rebrand all 9" (Scenario 1): near-identical monthly cost (~AED 288K vs 285K) but it avoids opening a Modern Vet 800 m from our own Hospital; the repurposed Al Wasl unit adds a service line instead of cannibalising one.

Headline numbers (S4): group marketing run-rate AED ~288K/month (+65K, +29%), reached by month 9 — one hire (Social Media Manager) plus ~AED 6K/month of raises for the existing team, who take on the new sites; one-off integration ~AED 590K over 12 months. Marketing spend falls to 2.7–3.1% of combined revenue (3.4% today; board corridor 3.0–3.5%). Per-clinic marketing cost drops from AED 20.3K to ~14.4K/month.

Five things the board should know

1 · It's a footprint deal, not a brand deal.

City Vet's value to us is geography: East Dubai (Mirdif, Al Warqa, DSO — zero Vet Alliance presence today), Al Barsha Mall, and first-mover entry into Abu Dhabi and Al Ain. Our April 2026 Abu Dhabi expansion report scored Khalifa City as the reference "nearest vet" for every opportunity zone — City Vet already owns that spot.

2 · Three of nine clinics sit on top of ours.

Al Wasl (0.8 km from MV Hospital), JVT (1.9 km from MV JVC), Meydan (2.6 km from MV Azizi Riviera). These need a decision each — merge, repurpose, or accept overlap — regardless of brand choice.

3 · Their digital assets are a liability to inherit, not an asset to protect.

Webflow site with 9 templated location pages (same H1, same phone, no meta descriptions), DR 20 vs our 54, ~229 organic visits/mo vs our ~4,400, Instagram ~16K vs Modern Vet 65K, no YouTube vs our 165K, no Meta/TikTok pixel, dead Shopify store, 2× Instagram, 2× Facebook, 3× LinkedIn pages. SEO risk of rebranding their domain into modernvet.com: low — worst case ~5% dilution of our organic traffic.

4 · Their reputation is good, ours is better.

4.52★ vs 4.74★ weighted. Weakest: Mirdif 4.3★ (lowest score) and Al Ain (thinnest footprint, closed Fridays, 12–9pm). One public negligence thread names the Al Wasl flagship. All Dubai branches would be in the bottom half of our estate on rating — a "Modern Vet standard" uplift programme is needed either way.

5 · Clinical assets worth keeping: CT scanner, endoscopy, exotics/avian/rabbit practice, wellness-plan book, 26 vets.

We don't have CT. Their exotics/rabbit line complements our "MV = dogs & cats" rule (today we route rabbits to BOV/VV). Their wellness plans (12-month, AED 150 admin fee) are an installed recurring-revenue base — migrating those members without churn is the single most important retention task in the first 90 days. Founder-led, no PE backing found → key-person risk and a founder whose name is on the brand: the brand conversation must be handled with him, not around him.

Four scenarios

All scenarios assume the same 9 clinics join and the same day-1 "part of Vet Alliance" endorsement. They differ in what sign goes on the door and, therefore, in what marketing has to run forever.

S1 · One Brand — all 9 become Modern Vet

Full rebrand in 3 waves over 9–12 months. One website, one ad account per platform, one social stack. Existing team runs the new sites (~10% raises), plus one Social Media Manager. Paid media (+30K) goes where Modern Vet has no presence: East Dubai and the two emirates.

Monthly marketing (group): AED 285K (+62K, +28%) · Roles: 15 (+1, plus raises) · One-off: ~AED 570K

+ Lowest run-rate, strongest brand, simplest team. Cannibalisation of Modern Vet's own organic footprint is small.
− Puts a Modern Vet 800 m from Modern Vet Hospital; highest change-load on clients and 26 vets at once; founder's brand disappears (deal sensitivity).

S2 · House of Brands — all 9 stay City Vet

City Vet continues as a third GP brand alongside Modern Vet and BOV, with "part of Vet Alliance" endorsement. No brand manager — the existing team owns it, with ~25% raises for PPC, SEO, design and content — but a second website needs its own SEO budget, its own production and collateral, separate ad accounts (+50K media), an operations specialist and a Social Media Manager. The brand has to be maintained whether we like it or not.

Monthly marketing (group): AED 347K (+124K, +56%) · Roles: 16 (+2, plus raises) · One-off: ~AED 145K

+ Cheapest year-1, lowest client disruption, keeps founder goodwill, preserves local names in East Dubai/AUH/Al Ain.
− Most expensive forever; we bid against ourselves on Google in Al Wasl, JVT, Meydan, Barsha; we'd be investing to fix a weaker brand's digital estate; no scale benefit to Modern Vet; management attention split three ways (four with Vienna Vet).

S3 · Hybrid (as proposed) — MV where we have no clinic, City Vet where we do

Rebrand Barsha, DSO, Al Warqa, Mirdif, Abu Dhabi, Al Ain to Modern Vet (6). Keep City Vet at Al Wasl, JVT, Meydan (3) to avoid two Modern Vets side by side.

Monthly marketing (group): AED 344K (+121K, +54%) · Roles: 16 (+2, plus raises) · One-off: ~AED 489K

+ Avoids "MV next to MV"; softer landing for the flagship and the founder.
− Same run-rate as keeping the whole brand, plus a rebrand one-off: a full second brand infrastructure kept alive for just 3 clinics; those 3 still compete with our own MV sites 0.8–2.6 km away — the overlap isn't solved, it's relabelled; two booking flows, two websites, two review programmes.

S4 · Recommended — Modern Vet everywhere, Al Wasl repurposed

Rebrand 8 to Modern Vet. Al Wasl flagship (800 m from the Hospital) is not run as a second GP clinic: options are (a) Vienna Vet #2 — cat-only, premium, matches the villa/Al Wasl demographic and the Saadiyat-type play we already like; (b) Modern Vet Wellness & Exotics Hub — grooming, wellness plans, avian/rabbit/exotic (their strength, our gap), day-care; (c) consolidate staff/equipment (CT!) into the Hospital and exit the lease. Choose after seeing the lease and the P&L.

Monthly marketing (group): AED 288K (+65K, +29%) · Roles: 15 (+1, plus raises) · One-off: ~AED 588K

+ Same run-rate as S1, no self-cannibalisation, adds a service line, uses the flagship's equipment and reputation, gives the founder's original site a distinct future.
− Slightly higher one-off (flagship refit), requires a clinical decision on Al Wasl the board must make early.

Recommendation and 12-month plan

Adopt Scenario 4. Decide the Al Wasl flagship's future in the first 60 days. Rebrand in three waves, East-to-West.
