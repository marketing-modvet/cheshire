The moment that matters most

When a patient passes, what happens next says a lot about the care a clinic provides. FarewellPet exists so that moment is handled with the same warmth and professionalism your clients trust you for — without adding it to your team's workload.

We are an independent pet cremation & aftercare service based in Dubai, built specifically to support veterinary clinics and the families they serve. We work with clinics across Dubai — including clinics with no other affiliation to us — because every clinic deserves a reliable, dignified aftercare partner.

How it works

1

You call

Your team contacts FarewellPet when a client needs aftercare arrangements — by phone, WhatsApp, or your usual channel

2

We collect

Scheduled pickup from your clinic, handled discreetly and respectfully, on your timeline

3

We care

Cremation (private/individual or communal, per the family's choice) and return of ashes or a memorial keepsake, if selected
