Commercial models

Terms that work for your clinic

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all pricing for partners. Here's how it typically works — final terms are agreed per clinic.

Flat partnership

A fixed monthly or annual arrangement, regardless of case volume — predictable for clinics with steady referral flow.

Per-case commission

A commission on each case referred — no fixed cost, scales naturally with your clinic's activity.

Hybrid

A smaller flat base plus a per-case commission — a middle ground many clinics prefer.
