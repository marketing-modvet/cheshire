Built for the moment a family says goodbye

FarewellPet exists for one purpose — to give every pet, and every family, a dignified end to their story together.

Where we are

A dedicated space, separate from clinical care

Our facility is located in Khawaneej, Dubai, with its own dedicated entrance — separate from any veterinary clinic. Families come to a calm, considered space built with one purpose: to care for a pet, and the people who loved them, at the end.

[photo placeholder — reception/waiting area]

Fully ready

Equipped, licensed, and compliant

Every legal and regulatory requirement in the UAE has already been met. Our facility and equipment are fully in place — built to the standard families and veterinary partners can trust without question.

Why "FarewellPet"

An independent name, on purpose

We chose to build FarewellPet as its own brand — not an extension of any single clinic — so every family and every veterinary partner can trust it as a dedicated, independent aftercare specialist, not an upsell.
