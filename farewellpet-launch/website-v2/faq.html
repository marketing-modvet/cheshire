About the process

Is pet burial legal in Dubai?

No — cremation is the only legally sanctioned pathway for pet aftercare in Dubai. We handle this in full compliance with UAE regulations.

Choosing an option

What if I'm not sure which option to choose?

That's completely normal. Reach out and we'll talk it through with you, at your pace — no pressure, no rush.

What's the difference between communal and private cremation?

Communal cremation is shared and no ashes are returned; private/individual cremation is done separately for your pet alone, with all ashes returned to you. See our Services page for a full comparison.
