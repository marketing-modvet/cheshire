Questions, answered gently

Is pet burial legal in Dubai?

No — cremation is the only legally sanctioned pathway for pet aftercare in Dubai. We handle this in full compliance with UAE regulations.

How long does the process take?

Timelines vary by the option you choose — we'll walk you through exactly what to expect when you reach out. [placeholder — confirm real timelines]

Can my veterinary clinic arrange everything for me?

Yes. Many of our partner clinics can arrange collection and options on your behalf, so you don't have to think about logistics.

What if I'm not sure which option to choose?

That's completely normal. Reach out and we'll talk it through with you, at your pace — no pressure, no rush.
