Good to know

Volunteer led

Hosts are colleagues, not coaches. The sessions are social, not about performance.

Bring family and friends

Children above 12 are welcome with the supervision of an adult. Add your guests when you book.

No pressure

Come to one session or all of them. Costs are minimal: a court fee or a bottle of water at most.

Stay in the loop

Reminders and any changes are shared in the Vet Alliance WhatsApp group.
