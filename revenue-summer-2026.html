Summary & Prognosis

July 2026 is pacing toward 6.4–6.5M vs 6.67M in June — a decline of 2.5–4.0% (5.7–7.1% on a daily-average basis, adjusting for July's extra day). This dip is a well-established seasonal pattern, visible in all three years of data.

The seasonal July dip is shrinking every year:
  • 2024: −12.0% (daily −14.8%)
  • 2025: −6.0% (daily −9.1%)
  • 2026: −2.5% to −4.0% (daily −5.7% to −7.1%)
Each year the summer trough gets shallower — demand is becoming more resilient to the low season.

YoY growth is accelerating. June 2026 grew +20.4% vs 2025 (which itself grew +9.4% vs 2024). July 2026 is projected at +23.0–24.9% YoY — meaning our "slowest" month this summer will exceed last July by roughly a quarter, and will be higher than any single month of summer 2025.

August prognosis: August has recovered from July in both prior years — +8.3% in 2024 and +4.1% in 2025. Applying that recovery range to the July projection gives August 2026 at 6.66–7.04M (daily average 215–227K), i.e. +23–30% YoY. The midpoint scenario (~6.8–6.9M) would make it the strongest month of the summer and likely a new record for an August.

Bottom line: July's softening is normal seasonality — milder than in either of the two prior years — while underlying YoY growth continues to strengthen quarter after quarter.
