Procedure Page Rewrites — Drafts for Approval

Title, meta, and H1 rewrites for the 6 procedure pages flagged in the AI Search Visibility audit. Side-by-side comparison: current (broken) vs proposed.

Rewrite rules used

  • Title: Procedure (clean English) + "Dubai" + "Modern Vet" / ModernVet · 50–60 characters · title case · no price-led phrasing
  • Meta: Complete sentence, 130–160 characters · includes procedure name, benefit, and brand · no fragments, no truncated phone numbers
  • H1: Procedure name (the way people search it) + "Dubai" · plain English · no marketing fluff slogans

1. Soft Tissue & Orthopedics

https://modernvet.com/our-services/soft-tissue-and-orthopedics/

❌ Current

Title
Orthopedic for Dogs & Cats: Vet Surgeon Specialist in Dubai
Meta
Orthopedic & Soft Tissue Surgery for Dogs & Cats in Dubai. Professional vet specialists at the Modern Vet clinic will take care of your pet 24/7
H1
Orthopedic Veterinary Dubai

✅ Proposed

Title
Orthopedic Surgery for Dogs and Cats in Dubai | Modern Vet
Meta
Veterinary orthopedic and soft tissue surgery in Dubai — TPLO, fracture repair, cruciate ligament, hip and joint procedures. 24/7 referral hospital at Modern Vet.
H1
Veterinary Orthopedic Surgery in Dubai
Why: ChatGPT lifted our description from a competitor's site, not ours. Current H1 reads as machine-translated ("Orthopedic Veterinary Dubai" = broken word order). New H1 puts the procedure name in natural English. Meta names the procedures (TPLO, cruciate) that AI chatbots specifically ask about.
