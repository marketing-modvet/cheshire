🎨 Brand identity (locked)

Logo

Circular emblem: a dog and cat silhouette side by side beneath a weeping willow branch (mourning/remembrance symbolism). Wordmark "Farewell" in serif above "PET FUNERAL SERVICES" in bold sans caps. Single coral/terracotta colourway on white.

Tone of voice

Warm, dignified, unhurried. Never clinical, never salesy. Grief-literate language — "goodbye," "remembrance," "care" over "disposal," "processing," "service fee." This tone has to carry through every touchpoint: website copy, Slack/email templates for grieving owners, even invoice wording.

4. B2B proposition (the main event)

Me can draft proposition, deck, outreach list & sequence Andrew/team for actual clinic-owner relationship calls

This is where most of the revenue pressure sits, per your instruction. The customer here isn't the grieving pet owner — it's every vet clinic in Dubai, including direct competitors of your own clinics. Two audiences within B2B:

Internal — the 11 Vet Alliance clinics

Day-one guaranteed volume. Needs: a simple internal SOP each clinic follows when a pet passes (who to call, what the owner is told, how pickup/paperwork works), staff briefing/training material, and the countertop collateral above. This is an operational rollout, not a sales pitch — should be the easiest and fastest piece to lock down.

External — competitor & independent clinics across Dubai

The real growth lever. Needs a genuine sales motion: a one-page proposition (what you offer, pricing/commission or referral-fee structure, pickup logistics, turnaround time, dignified packaging of ashes/certificates back to the clinic or owner), a short deck, and a target list to work through systematically.

What the external B2B pitch needs to say

  • Reliability: pickup on request, defined turnaround (competitors advertise 3–5 working days for ashes-back — match or beat it)
  • Logistics is a non-issue: your driver-dispatch/OptimoRoute fleet already handles scheduled pickups across Dubai (same rails used for lab samples/patient transport today) — this is a genuine, provable differentiator to lead with
  • Clear tiers: communal (no ash return), communal (ash return), private/individual — matches what the market already understands (confirmed from competitor research) so clinics don't need to re-educate their own clients
  • What's in it for the referring clinic: a referral fee/commission, or simply "we make your worst days easier and your client relationship looks better" — needs a decision from you on the commercial model
  • Trust signal that this is not "a competitor's cremation service" — standalone branding matters enormously here; the pitch should barely mention Vet Alliance up front
I can draft the full one-pager + short deck once pricing/commission structure is set. Target list build (every vet clinic in Dubai, contact info) is something I can research and compile now, independent of pricing.
