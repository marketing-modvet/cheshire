Headline

This is the strongest period since tracking began. Top 10 keyword count on mobile nearly doubled — 32 → 63 (out of 144 tracked) — and Top 3 went from 10 to 19. The flagship win: "vet near me" (vol 5,400) is now #3 on mobile, alongside a cluster of near-me terms at #3–#6.

Domain health also turned the corner: Ahrefs DR recovered 51 → 53 and referring domains jumped 838 → 1,005 (+167) — confirming the April–May dip was the Cloudflare glitch + spam-cleanup effect, not real decay. DR recovered to 53 by late May and has held; refdomains are at an all-time tracked high.

SE Ranking's priority-20 dashboard tells the same story: average position 24 → 14, traffic forecast 179 → 449 (+322), visibility 0.6 → 4.3, and 19 keywords up / 0 down in the latest movement window.
